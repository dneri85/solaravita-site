Good to know

Payment, scheduling, and travel

How to pay

We accept card, Zelle, cash, or check. Single sessions are paid at the end of each session. Packages are paid in full at your first session.

Need to reschedule?

Let us know at least 24 hours ahead, at no charge. Less than 24 hours' notice? The first time is on us. After that, there's a fee of half the session price. For packages, the fee is half the value of one package session. Illness and emergencies are always waived.

In-home visits

Travel fee per visit, based on where you live:

  • Margate and nearby citiesNo fee
  • Rest of Broward County$20
  • Nearby Palm Beach and Miami-Dade, up to about an hour from Margate$40

Nearby cities include Coconut Creek, Coral Springs, Parkland, Tamarac, North Lauderdale, and Pompano Beach. The fee applies to each visit, including package visits.

About packages

Use your three sessions within 60 days. If health or a family emergency gets in the way, we'll refund any unused sessions.
