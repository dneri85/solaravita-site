How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Tell us what feels hard

    Book a free conversation or give us a call. Share what you'd like to do, in your own words.

  2. Learn at your own pace

    We work on your own device, one step at a time, and repeat anything as often as you like.

  3. Leave feeling confident

    You'll go home with written steps and know what to do next time.

About Solara Vita

Light for life

Solara Vita means "Light for Life." We believe technology should make life easier, not more stressful, at every age.

We meet you where you are, explain things in plain language, and stay patient no matter how many questions you have. We use some automated tools behind the scenes to stay organized, but every person we help is supported by a real human being.

  • Clear explanations, no confusing jargon

Common questions

Questions people often ask

Do I need to know anything about technology to start?

Not at all. We start with what you already know and go from there. No question is too basic.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. Every service starts with a free consultation, so you can tell us what you need and decide if it's a good fit.

Can you help in person, or only online?

Both. We can meet online, or in person at your home or a community center when available in South Florida.

Can I book help for my mother, father, or someone I care for?

Yes. Many families reach out for a loved one. We can work with them directly, or with both of you together.

Should I bring my own phone or computer?

If you can, yes. Practicing on your own device helps new skills stick. If not, we'll still help.
